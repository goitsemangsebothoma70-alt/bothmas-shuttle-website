About Bothmas Shuttle

Bothmas Shuttle is a transportation service focused on providing safe, reliable and comfortable travel.

Our platform is being developed to connect passengers with registered drivers and vehicles through a simple digital booking system.

As the platform develops, passengers will be able to request trips while registered drivers will be able to manage their trips through their own driver portal.
